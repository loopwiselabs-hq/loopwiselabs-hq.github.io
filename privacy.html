Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy describes how Loopwise Labs ("we") handles information in connection with Glowlane, our YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account, and the software we use to run them (the "Glowlane Pipeline").

What we access

  • Public comments posted on our own videos and posts, including the commenter's public username or display name, the comment text and its time.
  • Aggregate statistics about our own videos and posts (for example views, likes and comment counts).
  • For our own TikTok account only: its basic profile information (display name, avatar and account ID) and the privacy options TikTok offers for its posts. We do not read TikTok comments or any information about other TikTok users.

We access this only for our own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account, through the official YouTube API Services, Instagram Platform APIs and TikTok for Developers APIs (Login Kit and Content Posting API), with permission granted by the account owner.

How we use it

  • To run the interactive formats: a public username may be shown in a later video (for example as a racer, as one of the first correct answers, or among the people who played), because the person commented to take part.
  • To reply publicly, from our own account, to the comment that got someone into a video, to let them know they are in it.
  • To publish our own videos to our own TikTok account. Each TikTok post is reviewed and approved by the account owner, who also chooses who can see it.
  • To measure how our content performs.

We filter out usernames that are offensive. We do not use this information for advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Storage and retention

We store only the minimum needed to run the next episode (usernames selected for a video, the IDs of the comments we reply to, and per-video statistics). Access tokens for our own accounts are kept in encrypted storage and are never shared. We do not collect email addresses, private messages or any non-public personal data from viewers.

Your choices

If you don't want your name to appear, simply don't comment, or delete your comment before the next daily episode. To ask us to remove your name from future videos, or not to reply to your comments, email loopwise.labs@gmail.com.

Google and YouTube

Glowlane uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our YouTube content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access granted to our app at Google security settings.

TikTok

Glowlane uses TikTok for Developers APIs only to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account. When you use TikTok, TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply. The account owner can remove our app's access at any time from the connected-apps settings of the TikTok app.
