Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy describes how Loopwise Labs ("we") handles information in connection with Comment Arena, our YouTube channel and Instagram account, and the software we use to run them (the "Comment Arena Pipeline").

What we access

  • Public comments posted on our own videos and posts, including the commenter's public username or display name, the comment text and its time.
  • Aggregate statistics about our own videos and posts (for example views, likes and comment counts).

We access this only for our own YouTube channel and Instagram account, through the official YouTube API Services and Instagram Platform APIs, with permission granted by the account owner.

How we use it

  • To run the interactive formats: a public username may be shown in a later video (for example as a racer or as one of the first correct answers), because the person commented to take part.
  • To measure how our content performs.

We filter out usernames that are offensive. We do not use this information for advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Storage and retention

We store only the minimum needed to run the next episode (usernames selected for a video and per-video statistics). We do not collect email addresses, private messages or any non-public personal data from viewers.

Your choices

If you don't want your name to appear, simply don't comment, or delete your comment before the next daily episode. To ask us to remove your name from future videos, email loopwise.labs@gmail.com.

Google and YouTube

Comment Arena uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our YouTube content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access granted to our app at Google security settings.
